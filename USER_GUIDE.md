## 🚀 Persona Workflow Tutorials

### 1. Technical Trainers: Grading & Attendance
* **Update Attendance Daily**:
  1. Click **Attendance** on the sidebar.
  2. Pick the target date and toggle logs status (Present, Absent, Leave).
  3. Click **Save Daily Sheet** to register metrics.
* **Grade Assessments**:
  1. Go to **Assessments**. Select a trainee.
  2. Click **Grade Weekly Test**, **Grade Assignment**, or **Grade Module Test** modal buttons.
  3. Input scores and comments, then save.

### 2. HR / L&D Managers: Placement & Analytics
* **Export Reports**:
  1. Go to **Trainees** directory or **Analytics**.
  2. Click **Export CSV** or **Export Excel** for spreadsheets, or **Export PDF** to generate branded corporate profiles.
* **Review Cohort Dashboard**:
  1. Go to **Analytics** to view live line trends, risk distributions, and module averages.

### 3. Department Heads: AI Insights Review
* **Check Cohort Health**:
  1. Login as Department Head.
  2. Read the **AI Executive Summary** on the main dashboard tab.
  3. Click **View Complete AI Intelligence** to see deep cohort reviews and placement recommendation tables.
